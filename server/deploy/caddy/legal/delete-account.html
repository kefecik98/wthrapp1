Delete your WeatherAlert account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open WeatherAlert and sign in.
  2. On the home screen, scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email {{env "SUPPORT_EMAIL"}} from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my WeatherAlert account. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything we hold about you: your email and sign-in details, your location, alert settings, alert history, push token and subscription record. Copies may remain in database backups for up to 14 days before those backups are deleted. Nothing is kept after that.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription — Google Play bills it, not us. Cancel it in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
