WeatherAlert Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

WeatherAlert warns you before rain, snow, hail, lightning or strong wind reaches where you are. To do that it needs your location, including while the app is closed. This page explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

WeatherAlert is operated by {{env "OPERATOR_NAME"}}. Questions: {{env "SUPPORT_EMAIL"}}.

What we collect

DataWhy
Approximate location — the grid square, about 3 km across, that your phone is in, and when it was last updated. Your phone works out the square itself: your exact GPS position never leaves your device. Collected in the background, while the app is closed, if you allow it. To check the forecast for your area and alert you before weather arrives. We keep only your latest grid square; each update overwrites the previous one. We do not keep a location history.
Account — your email address, and either a password (stored only as a one-way hash) or the account identifier Google provides when you sign in with Google. To sign you in and keep your settings.
Alert settings — which weather types you want, how far ahead, rain sensitivity, notifications on/off. To send only the alerts you asked for.
Alert history — the type of each alert we sent you and when. So we never send you the same alert twice.
Push token — an identifier for your device issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging. To deliver notifications to your phone.
Subscription status — whether you have Premium, which plan, and when it renews or expires. To unlock Premium features. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your card or bank details.
Server logs — IP address and request details. To keep the service running and to limit abuse. Logs are rotated and not used for anything else.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data.

Who else sees it

Our servers are run by us, and every connection to them is encrypted. We use these services to provide the app, and share only what each one needs:

  • Tomorrow.io (weather data) receives the centre point of the forecast area you are in — an area several kilometres across that is shared by everyone in it, never your own position — so it can return the forecast. Nothing identifying you is sent with it.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging receives your push token and the text of each alert, to deliver it to your device.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play handle subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your WeatherAlert account ID and your purchase records.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it, tells us your email and Google account identifier.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, everything above is deleted from our database immediately. Copies may remain in database backups for up to 14 days before those backups are themselves deleted.

Your choices

  • Location and notifications: you can turn either off at any time in your phone's settings. Alerts stop working without them.
  • Delete your account: in the app, tap Delete account on the home screen. If you can't use the app, see how to request deletion.
  • Access or correction: email us and we will send you a copy of your data or correct it.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are stored as salted one-way hashes. The database is not reachable from the internet.

Children

WeatherAlert is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
